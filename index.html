How it works

Every dose, verified in seconds.

A reminder on the patient's phone, a short dose video with a couple of e-diary questions, and a quick review where the care team sees the answers right next to the video.

  • 1Reminder
  • 2Record
  • 3E-diary
  • 4Review
  • 5Regimen
↓See the steps
↓Space next step ↑ back
1 / 8
Patient · 8:00 AM

A reminder arrives at dose time.

The phone shows a simple prompt: it's time for your video. Patients can record right away or snooze for 30 minutes.

  • Follows each patient's schedule
  • Follow-up if a video is missed
2 / 8
Patient

One tap opens today's dose.

The home screen shows what's due, with a picture of the medication and where they are in treatment.

  • Face ID or fingerprint to unlock
  • Pill images for each medication
3 / 8
Patient · a few seconds

They record themselves taking it.

A short selfie video captures the dose. It's encrypted on the phone, kept out of the camera roll, and saved if there's no signal.

  • Works offline
  • Deleted from the phone after upload
4 / 8
PatientE-diary

When did they last eat?

Right after the video, the app asks the e-diary questions the study needs. Mealtime comes first: the patient picks today or yesterday and the time of their last meal.

  • Questions set per study
  • Or a simple yes/no: ate in the hour before?
5 / 8
PatientE-diary

Was a treatment supporter there?

One tap answers whether a treatment support provider was with them. The video and the answers send together, and the patient is done.

  • One tap: Yes or No
  • Sends with the dose video
6 / 8
Care team · moments later

The care team is notified.

New videos land in a review queue. Late and missed doses rise to the top, so the team sees who needs attention first.

  • Email alerts for critical events
  • Two-way messaging with patients
7 / 8
Care team · about 10 secondsE-diary

The e-diary answers sit beside the video.

In the review, the patient's answers show under Reported details, right next to the dose video: when they last ate and whether a treatment supporter was there. The reviewer checks them, confirms the dose, and moves on.

  • Last meal with date and time
  • Reviewers can correct an answer
  • Confirmed doses flow to the EDC
8 / 8
Care team

Change the regimen once. The app follows.

When a schedule changes, the care team updates it in one place. The patient's app and reminders update to match, and every dose shows on the treatment calendar.

  • Synced to the patient app
  • Adherence at a glance
